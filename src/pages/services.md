We are an independent implementation agency for the Open Knowledge Format. We help teams convert operational knowledge into clean, agent-readable OKF bundles, built to Google Cloud's open v0.1 specification. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Google.

Four services, one clear path: start with an audit, then implement, migrate, or maintain.

## OKF Readiness Audit

**Who it is for:** Any team that wants OKF but is not yet sure what to convert or what it will cost.

**What we deliver:** A structured assessment of your knowledge sources, a prioritised conversion map, a recommended bundle structure, and a costed implementation plan.

**The outcome:** You know exactly what to convert, in what order, and what it will take, before committing budget to a build.

[Book an OKF readiness audit](/okf-readiness-audit/)

## OKF Starter Implementation

**Who it is for:** Teams ready to produce their first production OKF bundle set from a defined source.

**What we deliver:** A working bundle set built to spec, with valid YAML frontmatter, sensible `type` classification, reserved `index.md` and `log.md` files where appropriate, tagging, and validation.

**The outcome:** A real, usable OKF bundle in your repository that your team and agents can read straight away.

[Explore Starter Implementation](/okf-implementation/)

## OKF Knowledge Base Migration

**Who it is for:** Teams with a large or messy documentation set, support KB, or wiki to move into OKF.

**What we deliver:** A focused migration sprint that converts a substantial knowledge base into clean, version-controlled OKF directories, with structure, tagging, deduplication, and validation.

**The outcome:** A large body of knowledge, packaged consistently and ready for agents, without months of internal effort.

[See knowledge base migration](/okf-knowledge-base-migration/)

## OKF for AI Agents

**Who it is for:** AI product teams that want the context their agents read to be curated, portable, and accurate.

**What we deliver:** Agent-focused OKF bundles structured around how your agents query knowledge, plus guidance on keeping that context current.

**The outcome:** Curated, version-controlled context your agents can read without bespoke SDKs, following the LLM-wiki pattern OKF formalises.

[Explore OKF for AI agents](/okf-for-ai-agents/)
